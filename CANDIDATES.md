# Candidates — The 3 AM Excuse

Brief (judge criteria, in order): (1) an excuse a hardware wallet gives for
refusing to sign a transaction at 3 AM; (2) original angle — avoid the worn
jokes about batteries, firmware updates, cold storage; (3) the wallet's own
voice, a device with its own priorities and grudges; (4) the 3 AM hour itself
is the joke — the line could not be pasted onto a 3 PM transaction;
(5) one tight line under 200 characters, every word earning its place.

All candidates are original, written for this bounty. No entry text was copied
from the field: only public submission titles were visible, never entry texts.

## The ten candidates

| # | Chars | Line |
|---|-------|------|
| A | 110 | My secure element clocked out at midnight. At 3 AM the only one on duty is a screensaver, and he has no hands. |
| B | 107 | Last time you signed at 3 AM you bought a JPEG of a rock. I remember everything — that's why I'm saying no. |
| C | 103 | I have two buttons and at 3 AM both of them are asleep. Pressing harder won't wake them. It never does. |
| D | 77 | 3 AM is when my clock and your judgement both stop working. I trust my clock. |
| E | 104 | My seed phrase has 24 words and at 3 AM I can only remember 23 of them. Come back when we're both sober. |
| G | 75 | At 3 AM every address looks like a scam, including yours. Especially yours. |
| I | 103 | I'm not signing at 3 AM. The last thing I signed at 3 AM is still explaining itself to your accountant. |
| J | 105 | My buttons are on strike until sunrise. They refuse to sign a transaction that is clearly a cry for help. |
| K | 101 | 3 AM, full balance, unknown address, 'trust me'. I have one job, and it is saying no to exactly this. |
| L | 114 | I keep your life savings behind a four-digit PIN and you want decisions at 3 AM? My yes button is on annual leave. |

## Local judging (pairwise by the brief's criteria, in order)

- **B — PRIMARY (submitted).** (1) An explicit refusal, grounded in the
  wallet's job: it remembers. (2) The most original angle in the set: the
  wallet weaponises its perfect memory against its owner's worst 3 AM
  purchase — a JPEG of a rock, the crypto-native regret. No battery, firmware
  or cold-storage cliché anywhere. (3) Pure device voice: "I remember
  everything" is what a ledger does; a person forgets, a wallet doesn't.
  (4) 3 AM is load-bearing twice: the last bad signing happened at 3 AM, and
  the refusal happens now, at 3 AM, for exactly that reason. (5) 107 chars,
  no word cuttable; the payoff lands in the final four words.
- **A — runner-up.** Strong device voice (secure element, screensaver) and a
  vivid punch ("he has no hands"). Loses to B on (2): the off-duty shift
  worker is a more familiar trope than the wallet's grudge-memory, and the
  hour is named but the joke would survive at 2 AM.
- **K — third.** Tightest refusal ("I have one job"), and the 3 AM / full
  balance / unknown address / 'trust me' list is a sharp portrait of the exact
  transaction a wallet should refuse. Loses to B on (2): pattern-recognition
  is clever but less surprising than the rock JPEG, and "I have one job" is a
  borrowed phrase.
- **G.** Very tight (75 chars), paranoid-wallet voice, "Especially yours" is a
  good sting. But the refusal is implied rather than stated, and "everything
  looks like a scam" is a shorter joke with less of a story than B.
- **I.** "Still explaining itself to your accountant" is a lovely aftermath
  image and the refusal is explicit. Loses to B on (2): the accountant joke is
  a second punch that dilutes the wallet's voice, and the 3 AM repetition is
  doing less work than B's memory.
- **J.** "A transaction that is clearly a cry for help" is the best single
  phrase in the set after B's rock. Loses on (4): the hour is only implied by
  "until sunrise", and a strike is a human metaphor that softens the device
  voice.
- **D.** The tightest (77 chars) and the cleanest 3 AM twist — but it reads
  like a generic assistant's one-liner, not a hardware wallet speaking;
  weakest on (3).
- **L.** "My yes button is on annual leave" is a fresh image and the refusal
  is explicit, but the setup is long (114 chars) and the four-digit-PIN
  framing spends words before the joke arrives.
- **C.** Device-specific (two buttons) and funny, but "asleep" is the most
  common 3 AM trope in the field; weakest on (2).
- **E.** Funny, but the wallet "remembering" only 23 of 24 seed words is a
  person-voice joke — a wallet stores its seed, it doesn't forget it — and
  "we're both sober" puts the device in a bar, not on a desk; weakest on (3).

## Field note

The field is 733 submissions and growing. The brief explicitly warns against
battery, firmware-update and cold-storage jokes, so all three were avoided;
"asleep / sleep mode / do not disturb / come back in the morning" lines were
also avoided as the most predictable 3 AM trope.
